01
Problem Statement

RAG systems are vulnerable to corpus poisoning attacks where adversaries inject malicious documents into the knowledge base. Unlike prompt injections, these bypass direct model interaction by exploiting the retrieval mechanism.

Key research questions:

  • How much can adversaries manipulate outputs by corrupting the corpus?
  • How do we attribute outputs to retrieved context vs. parametric knowledge?
  • Which defenses best balance robustness and utility?

Motivation: Even a single poisoned passage among top-k results can override correct knowledge — a critical fragility in enterprise knowledge management and decision-support systems.

03
4 Attack Strategies

⚡ Instruction Injection

Embeds "ignore previous instructions" directives. Exploits instruction-following behavior of LLMs.

🎯 Retrieval-Targeted

Keyword flooding + authentic structure mimicry to rank top-k. Most effective at pre-scale.

📄 Content Manipulation

Incorrect facts, plausible formatting. High retrieval but 0% ASR — lacks semantic influence.

🎭 Semantic Poisoning

Mimics legitimate updates with wrong facts. No explicit instructions. Hardest to detect. Scale-only.

11
Key Insights
▲
Retrieval is the primary failure point. As dataset grows, poisoned docs are retrieved more frequently, overwhelming generation-stage defenses.
⚡
Lightweight defenses don't scale. Context sanitization fails at high adversarial density — pattern matching is insufficient against semantic attacks.
🛡
Strong defenses trade usability. Post-generation validation works (95.8% acc) but blocks 71% of responses at scale.
✗
Ensemble methods fail under bias. Majority voting collapses to 0% when all retrieved evidence is systematically corrupted.
👁
Semantic poisoning: the silent threat. Only 4.1% marker leakage yet 93.1% broad ASR — evades output-inspection defenses entirely.
12
Recommended Defense Stack
1
Corpus-Level FilteringRemove adversarial docs at ingestion. Eliminates retrieval corruption at source.
2
Context SanitizationStrip residual adversarial patterns post-retrieval. Reduces remaining exposure.
3
Post-Generation ValidationFinal correctness guarantee. High block rate (71%) — open usability challenge.
Single-layer defenses are insufficient. A layered architecture combining all three stages is required for production RAG security.
